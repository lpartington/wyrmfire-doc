Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email wyrmfire@partington.info and we'll get back to you.

It helps if you tell us which iPhone or iPad you're using, its iOS version (Settings › General › About), and what happened just before the problem.

Getting started

  1. Tap Find your egg, then tap the egg until it hatches.
  2. Name your hatchling and choose girl or boy.
  3. Follow the golden arrow to the Dragon Academy, where Master Orrin teaches lessons such as flying through rings and breathing fire.
  4. In the great hall, sit on the glowing cushion and answer maths questions. Every right answer earns a gem and helps your dragon grow, all the way to a Great Wyrm.
  5. Collect gems all over the land and spend them on new skins in the Book of Wyrmcraft.

Touch controls

Joystick
Push up to flap, pull down to dive, push left or right to bank and turn.
FIRE
Hold to breathe fire.
WALK
Hold to walk on the ground, or to glide down and land.
SLEEP
Sleep on dry land to heal. Tap again to wake.
♥ COURT
Appears when a calm dragon is nearby. Court it and it becomes your mate, and you'll have an egg.
★ FRIEND
Appears next to a classmate in the great hall. Tap it to make friends.
Book
Opens the Book of Wyrmcraft. The world waits while the book is open.

Questions

Does Wyrmfire need the internet?

No. The whole game is on your device and works offline, including in aeroplane mode.

What does the game remember?

Your dragon's name, girl or boy, colour, the skin it's wearing, the skins you've unlocked and your best score. Each new game starts from a fresh egg.

I deleted the app and lost my skins.

Your progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes it. We don't keep a copy anywhere.

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. There are no in-app purchases or ads. Gems are earned in the game and can't be bought.

How do I swim?

Land on water, then pull the joystick down to dive. Watch your air, unless you're a Sapphire dragon.

My dragon is hurt.

Land on dry ground and tap SLEEP to heal.
