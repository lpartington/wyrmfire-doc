Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Wyrmfire does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases and no chat. The game works entirely offline and does not send anything over the internet.

Who we are

Wyrmfire is an iPhone and iPad game made by Teresa Partington (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, email wyrmfire@partington.info.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices between games, Wyrmfire saves a few settings in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your dragon's name and whether it is a girl or a boy
  • your dragon's colour and the skin it is wearing
  • the skins you have unlocked in the Book of Wyrmcraft
  • your best score

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Wyrmfire has no servers and contains no third-party code that collects data, so we never receive personal information from the app.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics. These do not identify you, we use them only to fix problems in the game, and Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple collects them. You can turn this off in the same settings.

Children

Wyrmfire is designed to be enjoyed by children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It has no advertising, no links out of the app and no way to contact other players.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
